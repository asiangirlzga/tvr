# Pairing your TV

Pairing connects the phone app to your TV once. After that the TV remembers the phone.

**Nothing needs to be installed on the TV.** The code screen is built into Android TV / Google TV.
Install the app on the phone only.

## What you need

- A TV running **Android TV or Google TV** (not Samsung, LG, Roku or Fire TV).
- The TV is **turned on** and connected to Wi-Fi.
- The phone is on the **same Wi-Fi network** as the TV. No Bluetooth is used.

## Steps

1. Open **Voice TV Remote** on the phone.
2. Pick your TV under **TVs on your network**. If it doesn't appear, type the TV's IP address
   (TV *Settings → Network*) under **Add by IP address** and tap **Pair**.
3. The TV shows a **6-character code** on its screen.
4. Type the code into the app and tap **Pair**.
5. Done. The remote opens, and next time you can tap the TV in the **Paired TVs** list.

## If something goes wrong

| Problem | Try this |
| --- | --- |
| TV isn't listed | Check both devices are on the same Wi-Fi (not a guest network). Turn the TV on, then wait a few seconds. Or add it by IP address. |
| "Couldn't reach the TV" | TV may be off or asleep. Turn it on and retry. |
| "That code doesn't match" | Re-check the code on the TV (letters A–F and numbers). It is not case-sensitive. |
| No code appears on the TV | Close the pairing box in the app and start pairing again. |
| "The TV didn't accept this phone. Pair it again." | Tap **Pair again** in the app. This can happen after a TV factory reset. |
| TV's IP changed | The app follows the TV by name when it appears in the list. A fixed (reserved) IP in your router avoids this. |

## Removing a phone

Tap the bin icon next to the TV in **Paired TVs** to forget it in the app. You can pair again at any time.
